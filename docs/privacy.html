Thunderbolt Budget

Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026

The short version

  • Thunderbolt Budget does not collect, store or share any personal data.
  • Everything the app shows is read from your Mac and stays on your Mac.
  • The app has no network access, so it can't send data anywhere.
  • There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

What the app reads

To show which devices are connected to which port and how much bandwidth they use, Thunderbolt Budget reads the following from macOS while it is running:

  • Connected hardware: names, manufacturers, model and vendor identifiers, serial numbers and connection speeds of Thunderbolt, USB4 and USB devices and connected displays.
  • Display details: resolution, refresh rate and the information displays report about themselves (such as manufacturer and manufacture date).
  • Traffic counters: the total number of bytes read from and written to external drives, and sent and received by external network adapters. The app only reads these totals to calculate speed. It never sees the contents of your files or network traffic.

This information is held in memory only while the app is open. It is not saved to disk, not sent to the developer and not shared with anyone.

What the app stores

The app saves only your settings on your Mac: the bottleneck alert threshold, whether alerts are on, the refresh rate and your appearance choice. These stay in the app's own sandboxed storage on your Mac and are never sent anywhere.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app shows bottleneck alerts on your Mac. They are created on your Mac and are not sent through any server. You can turn them off in the app's Settings or in System Settings ▸ Notifications.

Copying to the clipboard

If you choose to copy your device list, it is placed on your Mac's clipboard. What happens to it after that is up to you.

Network access

Thunderbolt Budget runs in Apple's App Sandbox without permission to make or accept network connections. It contains no third-party code, analytics or advertising frameworks.

Crash reports from Apple

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in macOS (System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Analytics & Improvements), Apple may send the developer anonymous crash and performance reports. Apple controls this, and you can turn it off at any time in that setting. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
